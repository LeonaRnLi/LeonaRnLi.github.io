Selected Projects

MobiCom 2025

HydraNet: Spatial Reuse for LoRa Networks

Developed an interference-tolerant system that enables simultaneous LoRa transmissions on the same frequency. Designed a LoRaWAN-compatible protocol combining beamforming, power control, and scheduling, and built a full-stack testbed with USRP gateways and commodity LoRa nodes.

GitHub ↗   Paper ↗

UAV Communications

Cellular Infrastructure for BVLOS UAV Operations

Investigates timely and reliable cellular communication for beyond-visual-line-of-sight UAV missions. Built an end-to-end prototype integrating cellular modems, onboard computers, PX4, and MAVLink; studied multi-link communication and traffic management through real-world flight experiments.

UAV Navigation

Infrastructure-Assisted Navigation in GNSS-Denied Environments

Explores how ground-based wireless infrastructure can assist UAV localization when GNSS is unavailable. Develops localization and navigation strategies to maintain position awareness and guide UAVs toward GNSS recovery.

SIGCOMM 2026

Planet-Scale IoT Connectivity via LEO Satellites

Contributed to research on planet-scale IoT connectivity through low-Earth-orbit satellites, including experimental measurements and analysis of satellite communication links.

GitHub ↗

SIGCOMM 2025

AlphaWAN: Scalable and Coexisting LoRaWANs

Investigated decoder contention as a bottleneck in operational LoRaWANs and contributed to measurement analysis and channel planning for scalable connectivity and spectrum sharing among coexisting networks.
